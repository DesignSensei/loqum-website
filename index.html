HOW LOQUM WORKS

Everything you need for cleaner locum staffing

Verified Profiles

Every pharmacist on Loqum goes through profile verification before taking shifts. Pharmacies can review key details before confirming cover, while pharmacists build a trusted work profile over time.

Post and Manage Shifts

Pharmacies can create shift requests with the date, time, location, rate, and role details clearly stated. This keeps staffing needs organized instead of relying on scattered calls and messages.

Match With Pharmacists

Loqum helps pharmacies connect with available pharmacists based on shift needs, location, rate, and availability.

Booking, Payment and Invoice Records

Once a shift is confirmed, Loqum keeps the booking details in one place. After the shift, payment and invoice records help reduce manual follow-up and keep both sides aligned.

What is Loqum?

Loqum is Nigeria's locum pharmacy staffing marketplace. Pharmacies post shifts, verified pharmacists apply, employers confirm the right locum, and both sides keep clearer shift records.

How does Loqum verify pharmacists?

Every pharmacist on Loqum goes through a professional identity verification process, including validation of their PCN registration. Only verified professionals can take confirmed shifts.

How do rates and commission work?

Pharmacies post shifts with clear rates and hours. Pharmacists see the details before applying. Loqum is free to join, and commission applies to pharmacists when they earn from a completed or confirmed shift.

How are shift records handled?

Once a shift is confirmed, Loqum keeps the booking details in one place. Check-in, clock-out, shift status, payment details, and invoice records help both sides stay aligned after the shift.

Is Loqum available outside Lagos?

Loqum is currently in early access and launching in Lagos first. We plan to expand to other Nigerian cities, so you can join the waitlist to be notified when Loqum reaches your area.
