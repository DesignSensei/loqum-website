FEATURE FAQ

Questions about Loqum features

Learn how Loqum helps pharmacies book verified locum pharmacists, protect shift payments, track attendance, and keep staffing records clearer.

How does Loqum verify locum pharmacists?

Pharmacists complete a professional profile and submit the details needed for verification. Loqum reviews their identity, professional information, and relevant registration details before they can take confirmed shifts.

What are Protected Shift Payments?

Protected Shift Payments help both sides work with more confidence. A pharmacy can fund a confirmed shift before work begins, while the pharmacist gets clearer assurance that the agreed payment is tied to the shift record.

How does check-in and clock-out work?

Pharmacists check in when they arrive for a shift and clock out when the shift ends. This gives the pharmacy and the pharmacist a clearer record of actual work time for reviews, payment confirmation, and dispute handling.

Can pharmacies review pharmacists before confirming cover?

Yes. Pharmacies can review available pharmacist profiles, verification status, location, availability, work history, and trust signals before choosing who to confirm for a shift.

What records does Loqum keep after a shift?

Loqum keeps key shift information in one place, including the booking details, agreed rate, shift status, check-in and clock-out records, payment status, and invoice records where applicable.
